How to play

The rules

  • Run forward automatically. Reach the end of the course before time runs out.
  • At a fork, choose: left is the safe route, right is the risky route where fragments count double.
  • Jump gaps - falling in ends the run. Slide under vine barriers - each hit costs a heart.
  • Steer between lanes to collect relic fragments. Three of a kind earn a set bonus.
